Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Dory is a desktop app that runs on your own Mac. When you connect your Google account, Dory reads your Gmail, Calendar and Drive read-only, on your Mac, and stores what it keeps encrypted on your Mac. Nothing is sent to the developer's servers; there are none.

To turn text into memories, Dory may send parts of it to the AI model provider you configure, under your own API key and that provider's terms.

You can disconnect Google at any time in Dory or at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete everything Dory keeps from the app.

Dory's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
